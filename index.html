01 / About

Curiosity turns into working systems.

I am interested in the space where electronics, firmware and software meet.

A/01
Approach

Build. Measure. Understand.

I enjoy understanding how a system behaves from the circuit level up: what a GPIO pin is doing, how a motor driver responds, how wireless commands reach a controller and how software turns those signals into useful behaviour.

My approach is hands-on and iterative. I break a problem into smaller parts, test each part, read the results and refine the complete system.
