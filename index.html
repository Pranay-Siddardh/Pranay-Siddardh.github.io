THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Learning by
looking deeper.

I’m an engineering student driven by curiosity about the systems that shape the world around us.

My interests move between practical electronics, mathematical foundations, circuit analysis and programming. I enjoy taking an idea apart conceptually, understanding the principles behind it, and then exploring how those principles can become something tangible.

This portfolio is a record of that process: projects, experiments, technical explorations and questions worth investigating.
